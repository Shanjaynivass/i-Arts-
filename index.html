Portraits That Speak Emotion

Short Introduction

Hi, I'm Shanjay Nivass
I'm a self-taught portrait artist who brings emotions to life through pencil, digital, and charcoal mediums.
With every stroke, I aim to capture the soul of a moment — whether it's a memory, a face, or a feeling.
Art is not just what I do, it's who I am.

I’m a passionate portrait artist from Tamil Nadu, India, and the creator of @i_arts.

From a young age, I’ve been drawn to the subtle beauty of faces—the way expressions tell stories words never could. Through pencil, digital, and charcoal mediums, I strive to bring emotions to life with fine detail and realism.

While I enjoy experimenting with different techniques, my heart lies in creating portraits that feel alive—whether it's a soft pencil sketch or a vibrant digital piece. Many of my works are inspired by people close to me, whose expressions have become a recurring muse in my journey.

Art, to me, is more than just skill—it's emotion, memory, and storytelling in visual form. Every portrait I create is a reflection of both the subject and a part of myself.

If you're someone who loves heartfelt, realistic art—or you're looking for a custom portrait made with care—I'm always open to connect and create.

Featured Artworks

Pencil Art

Pencil drawing is the heart of my art journey — it’s where I found my true connection with portraits.
I love how something as simple as a pencil can bring out deep emotions, lifelike details, and silent stories. Each portrait I create is drawn with patience and precision — capturing everything from a soft smile to a powerful gaze.

View Pencil Collection

Digital Art

Digital art gives me the freedom to blend realism with creativity — it’s where tradition meets technology in my portrait work.
I use digital tools not just for convenience, but to explore new styles, colors, and textures that go beyond paper. Every digital portrait I create still follows the same core principle I believe in: capturing emotion. Whether it's a soft skin tone, a glowing light, or a tiny sparkle in the eyes, I use digital brushes and layers to bring characters to life — with precision and personality.

View Digital Collection

Photo Recreations

One of my favorite things to do as an artist is to recreate photographs into hand-drawn portraits — capturing not just the image, but the emotion behind it.
My goal is to transform a simple photo into a meaningful piece of art that feels personal, emotional, and timeless.
Whether it’s a memory of a loved one, a gift for someone special, or a photo you deeply connect with, I aim to turn it into a portrait that feels alive. Every recreated artwork is done with care, and emotional depth — because I know the story behind a photo is just as important as the art itself.

View Photo Recreations

Anime To Live Action

As an artist, I’ve always been inspired by the emotion, detail, and personality found in anime characters. Through my “Anime to Live-Action” series, I take those iconic styles and transform them into realistic human portraits — imagining how these characters would look in real life.
Using digital techniques, I focus on reinterpreting facial features, expressions, and textures while staying true to the character’s soul. It’s a mix of fan art and realism — where fantasy meets reality.
This process allows me to explore new styles and connect deeply with anime fans through my own artistic lens.

About Me – Shanjay Nivass S

About Me

Hi, I’m Shanjay, the artist behind @i_arts — and welcome to my world of portrait art.

Art has always been more than a hobby for me. It’s a language — a way to express emotions, memories, and stories that words can’t fully capture. What started as simple pencil sketches has grown into a deep passion for creating realistic portraits through pencil, digital, and charcoal art.

My focus is on people and emotions. I love studying faces — every smile, every shadow, every sparkle in the eye. I believe portraits are more than just drawings; they’re moments frozen in time.

My Style

  • Pencil Art: Raw, emotional, and expressive. My first love and still my favorite.
  • Digital Art: Clean, colorful, and modern — perfect for creative expression and custom portraits.
  • Charcoal Art: Bold and deep, capturing dramatic contrasts and pure emotion.
  • Photo Recreations: I turn your favorite photos into timeless artwork.
  • Anime to Live Action: A personal series where I reimagine anime characters as real-life portraits.
